When an experiment grows, it moves to PrayogX

Prayog means experiment. Ideas start here as free, open experiments. The ones that need a team and a way to pay for themselves become products at PrayogX, built on the same rules: no ads, no selling data, no tricks to keep you hooked.

The first is Sehat, which keeps your family’s health records organised on WhatsApp. It’s in private beta.
