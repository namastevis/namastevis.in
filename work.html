How three projects came together: the data I started with, what I made from it, and where to see it.

District Nutrition Profile 2.0

Data design for IFPRI’s POSHAN initiative

The problem
District officials need a clear, consistent picture of nutrition in their district, and India has more than 700 districts. Designing each report by hand doesn’t scale.
What I built
A Python publishing pipeline that turns the data into a standard report card for every district, in the same layout, so any two can be compared side by side.
See it

8,477: the hidden data story of Indian Railways

Data story

The data
A 122-page government PDF listing every station on the network. Useful information, locked in a format nobody can explore.
What I made
I turned the PDF into a clean dataset of all 8,477 stations, checked 128 facts about inequality, geography and language, and told the findings as a story you scroll through.

Indian land measurement units

Interactive tool

The problem
A bigha in one state isn’t a bigha in another. Families, buyers and students struggle to compare land across India’s dozens of traditional units.
What I made
A mobile-friendly tool that converts every unit to one standard, square feet. The community helps add and correct units.
