About

I started in mathematics, moved into computer science, and found design by accident. Now I work where all three meet.

I studied applied mathematics at the Central University of Jharkhand, then computer science at IIIT Bhubaneswar. A research programme at the IITB-Monash Research Academy, between the IDC School of Design at IIT Bombay and computer science at Monash, is where design took over.

Since then I’ve taught computer science at ITER, and design at FLAME University’s School of Design, Art & Performance, with courses in creative programming, data visualisation and data-driven design.

What I believe

India produces huge amounts of public data, and most of it sits in PDFs nobody reads. I want to turn it into things people can use, and build products in India that the world can use too.

I practise free and open-source software. What I make for the public is open here. Products that need a team move to PrayogX.

Everything I build follows the same rules: the person using it comes first. No ads, no selling data, and no tricks to keep you hooked.
